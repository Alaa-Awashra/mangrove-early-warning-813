# Preregistered analysis plan — 2026-10-08 UTC

## Research question
Can pre-degradation hyperspectral observations predict persistent mangrove degradation before conventional multispectral monitoring detects it, and can those predictions identify high-blue-carbon areas for earlier intervention?

## Candidate and cohort rules
- Search EnMAP L2A, PRISMA L2D, and EMIT L2A metadata, favoring Arab-region mangroves. Select by accessible data, complete 24-month Sentinel-2 follow-up, usable native support, positive samples and spatially distinct events; never by model performance. Minimum for a limited PoC: 300 labelable supports, 30 persistent positives, 3 distinct patches. Prefer 1,000/50/5. Use one scene first.
- `t0` is the hyperspectral acquisition UTC time. The primary future horizon is **(t0, t0 + 24 calendar months]**. Reject incomplete horizons. Predictor acquisitions must be at or before t0; labels strictly after it.
- Use a Global Mangrove Watch extent epoch and version no later than t0. A native hyperspectral support enters the candidate cohort when at least **75%** of its footprint is mapped mangrove. EnMAP/PRISMA native image pixels are units; EMIT native GLT detector supports are units, with one deterministic lowest-row/column orthorectified representative per support chosen before outcome inspection. Supports are spatially correlated observations, not independent biological replicates.
- Aggregate valid Sentinel-2 L2A pixels intersecting each support using a median; require at least **50%** valid spatial coverage per date. Retain SCL 4/5/7 for canopy trajectories and track SCL 6 and MNDWI-positive pixels as water. Reject SCL 0/1/2/3/8/9/10/11. Use reflectance offsets/scales supplied with each product.

## Frozen trajectory and outcome rules
- Reference window: **[t0 − 24 months, t0 − 6 months]**, at least **6** valid observations spanning **9 months**. For each monitored date use a ±45-day day-of-year pre-t0 reference if at least **3** observations, otherwise the overall reference median and flag fallback. Reference NDVI variability is median absolute deviation (MAD), pre-t0 only. NDMI is diagnostic.
- Pre-t0 water baseline is the median and MAD of valid-date support water fraction and MNDWI. Mark a date a canopy water anomaly if its water fraction exceeds its own baseline median by **max(0.25, 3 × 1.4826 × MAD)**. Exclude that date from canopy NDVI trajectory, retain it for structural loss diagnostics. The 0.25 floor represents a large 25-percentage-point coverage change; the robust term adapts to local tide variability. Sensitivity: repeat with floors 0.20 and 0.30 without changing the primary rule.
- A valid canopy observation is degraded when **NDVI_observed − NDVI_reference ≤ −max(0.10, 2.5 × 1.4826 × MAD_reference)**. The 0.10 floor is a substantial absolute NDVI drop; the robust term protects naturally variable supports. This is an operational remote-sensing threshold, not a field diagnosis. Reference: [mangrove vegetation anomaly example](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/18/11/5456); [Sentinel-2 SCL documentation](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/APIs/openEO/openeo-community-examples/python/30_openeo-processing/scl-dilation-mask/scl-dilation-mask.html).
- Structural loss-to-water observation: post-t0 water fraction ≥ **0.50** and increase above the support's pre-t0 median ≥ **0.40**. This demands a dominant water footprint plus a major within-support shift, rather than treating ordinary tidal water as loss. It is a diagnostic subtype and can contribute to the combined primary endpoint.
- Persistent degradation: at least **2** degraded canopy or structural observations at least **30 days** apart, and at least **60%** of valid observations from the first degraded observation through horizon end degraded. If later recovery breaks 60%, classify Transient Stress. A structural subtype needs at least two qualifying structural observations; otherwise a persistent canopy sequence is canopy-dieback-like. The 30-day and 60% conditions are conservative operational persistence filters, fixed before counting.
- Stable: at least **6** valid future observations spanning **9 months**, no persistent sequence, and at most **1** isolated degraded observation. Transient Stress: degraded evidence without persistence and sufficient temporal coverage; exclude from binary modeling. Unresolved: inadequate coverage or ambiguous evidence; never map to Stable.
- `T_onset` is the first degraded date in the eventual qualifying episode. `T_detect` is the first date at which two degraded observations ≥30 days apart and ≥60% degradation among valid observations since onset are established using only observations available by that date. Save `T_last_ok` and the bracketing observation interval. A case is an early warning only if its **frozen t0 alert** preceded `T_detect`.
- Apply the identical detector to pre-t0 monitoring **(t0 − 6 months, t0]** against the frozen reference. Exclude any support detected by t0 from the primary at-risk cohort. Missing near-t0 Sentinel-2 F1 state uses [t0−60 days,t0] only if [t0−30 days,t0] has no valid date; flag fallback.

## Predictors and modeling
- F1: near-t0 Sentinel-2 B02/B03/B04/B05/B06/B07/B08/B8A/B11/B12 and NDVI, NDMI, NDRE, CIre, MNDWI. F2: pre-t0 NDVI/NDMI slope, MAD, 10th percentile, seasonal amplitude and valid count. F3: pre-t0 mangrove fraction, edge/water distance and suitable pre-t0 DEM when available. All predictor builders receive t0 and reject future dates.
- H1: full L2A spectrum after product QA, excluding invalid reflectance, officially bad bands, strong water-vapor intervals and nonphysical values. Retain a fixed band when predictor-side missingness is ≤ **5%**; use training-fold median imputation. For EnMAP, exclude the officially disabled 1331–1448 and 1796–1938 nm ranges and any product flagged bands; handle VNIR/SWIR overlap by choosing the VNIR band below 1000 nm and SWIR band at/above 1000 nm. [EnMAP L2A processor](https://www.enmap.org/data/doc/EN-PCV-TN-6007_Level_2A_Processor_Atmospheric_Correction_Land.pdf).
- H2: convolve the *same* HSI acquisition with official Sentinel-2A MSI spectral response functions by wavelength-weighted trapezoidal integration, with coverage checks; calculate broadband indices. This is a spectral-resolution control only. If official SRFs are unavailable, report H2 unavailable; no Gaussian substitute.
- H3: compact literature-verified physiological features only where wavelengths support them; document formulas/sources before use. H4 (Tier 2): H1 restricted to 400–1700 nm for spectral transferability only, without claiming 20 m spatial equivalence.
- Build ≥1 km spatial blocks, increasing size to the conservatively rounded pre-t0 empirical correlation scale where estimable. Grouped five-fold CV if each test fold has positive patches, otherwise grouped three-fold CV; report blocks and patch distribution. Use a spatial buffer where feasible without eliminating the training cohort. All imputation, scaling, PCA, regularization, class weighting, calibration and thresholds are outer-training-only. H1 logistic branch uses at most **10** PCA components, capped by training rank. Inner grouped CV chooses logistic C from **{0.01, 0.1, 1}**. Use training-only class weights.

## Hypotheses and evaluation
- **Confirmatory primary hypothesis:** regularized Logistic Regression C (F1–F3 + H1) improves pooled spatial out-of-fold PR-AUC over A (F1–F3). Report **PR-AUC(C) − PR-AUC(A)** with a paired **1,000-resample spatial-block bootstrap 95% CI**. Do not claim improvement if the interval includes zero.
- Secondary/exploratory: B (H1 only), D (F1–F3 + H2), E (F1–F3 + H3), F (F1–F3 + H4), a fixed modest nonlinear model, CNN HSI-only and CNN + baseline (≥3 seeds), and dieback-only sensitivity. C vs D tests spectral richness. No model or AOI selection by held-out performance.
- Report pooled OOF PR-AUC, ROC-AUC, Brier score, calibration and fold-level metrics. Alert budgets: **top 10% primary**, **top 5% and 20% secondary**. Per outer fold derive each frozen score threshold solely from inner grouped OOF scores on outer training, then apply once to outer test. Count true/false t0 alerts, positive patches alerted, `T_detect − t0` and HSI-added cases at equal budgets.
- Evaluate post-model blue-carbon targeting with a verified pre-t0 quantitative carbon raster if available; otherwise label the layer a **carbon-relevance proxy** and never report Mg C. Carbon is excluded from every degradation model. At 5/10/20% intervention budgets compare C-risk × carbon, A-risk × carbon, carbon-only and 1,000 random rankings using OOF predictions. Use training-only calibration where feasible; report proxy-weighted capture if only a proxy exists. Maps from full-cohort final models are visualization only; evaluation is OOF.
